Delivered by owl, at some expense

Dearest friend,

A very happy birthday to you. The owl was told to find the loveliest witch in the castle, and it flew straight to you without once asking for directions. Twenty-five on the nineteenth of November, and — if the enchantment on this page is working properly — still the sort of person who reads a letter all the way to the end.

You should know that two gifts have been hidden for you, and neither will simply be handed over. The castle has standards. There are examinations.

Pass your Potions and the first is yours. Fair warning: it is, technically, a love potion. You have never needed one, which is precisely why it is only a gift. Pass Herbology, which is longer and more stubborn than it sounds, and the second will bloom for you.

Take your time. Nobody is marking the clock. Wear something aesthetic while you work, though I have yet to see you in anything that wasn't.

with love,

The first gift

La Belle Rosea

Jean Paul Gaultier

Officially this is a love potion and therefore contraband, so please don't mention where you got it. Unofficially it is a perfume, it is rose-pink, it wears gold roses, and even its name means rosy. It was chosen because it looked as though it already belonged to you.

One gift down. The second is harder, and it is alive, which means it will not be rushed.

The Third Enchantment — The Relic Hunt

Seven things, badly hidden

Seven small objects have been tucked into the corners of everything you have already walked past: a star, a feather, a key, a phial, a rose, an acorn, a moon. They are faint, and they glimmer when you get near.

Scroll back up through the whole castle and tap every one you find. They are all on this page, tucked into the chambers you have already walked through. Stuck? Tap hint once for a nudge, and again to make the next one glow.

 

Herbology — Practical

Now the patience

All three enchantments are broken and the seed is in the soil. It grows at its own speed and not at yours. Water it, wait, water it again.

 

The second gift

A peony, framed

grown under glass, one petal at a time

Peonies are the show-offs of the flower world. They take years to settle before they bloom properly, they collapse dramatically in the rain, and they are worth every bit of the trouble. It seemed fitting.

This one is not on the screen. There is a real one, in a real frame, and it is waiting somewhere in the actual world for you to be handed it. Consider this your official notice that it exists.
